Privacy Policy for uploader

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the uploader application ("uploader", "the app"), operated by Prima Digital ("we", "us"), handles information when it is used to access Google Drive through Google's OAuth authorization and the Google Drive API.

1. Who uses this app

uploader is a private, single-user utility. It is used only by its owner, on the owner's own computers and servers, to manage the owner's own files. It is not a public service and no third-party users are registered or served by it.

2. Information the app accesses

When the owner authorizes uploader with Google OAuth, the app receives an access token for the Google Drive scope the owner approved. Using that token, the app can:

  • List, download, upload, rename, and delete files in the owner's Google Drive, only within the folders the owner directs it to use.
  • Read basic file metadata (file names, sizes, and modification times) needed to perform those file operations.

The app does not access the owner's email messages, contacts, location, or any other Google services. The app does not collect information about any person other than its owner.

3. How information is used

Accessed files and metadata are used solely to carry out the file operations the owner configured: automated backups of the owner's own configuration and project files to the owner's Google Drive, and retrieval or distribution of those files back to the owner's own machines. No data is used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or any purpose unrelated to operating the owner's own file management tasks.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share Google user data with any third party. Data is transmitted only between the owner's machines and Google Drive, over encrypted connections, as part of the operations described above. The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Storage and security

OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own machines, in configuration files with restricted permissions. No copy of the owner's Google Drive data is kept on any third-party server by this app beyond the owner's own devices and the owner's own Google Drive account.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens remain stored only while the owner continues to use the app. The owner may revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately invalidates the stored tokens. Files in Google Drive remain under the owner's full control and can be deleted by the owner at any time.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
