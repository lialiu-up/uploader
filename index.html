uploader

uploader is a small, single-user utility operated by Prima Digital. It helps its owner upload, download, back up, and manage files stored in the owner's own Google Drive account.

What uploader does

uploader connects to Google Drive through Google's official OAuth sign-in and the Google Drive API (via the open-source tool rclone). It is used for personal file management tasks such as automated configuration backups and distributing the owner's own project files between the owner's computers and Google Drive.

Who can use it

uploader is a private tool. It is not offered to the public, has no public sign-up, and is only ever authorized by its owner on the owner's own devices.

Your data

uploader only ever accesses files that its owner selects. It does not sell data, does not share data with advertisers, and does not collect data about other people. Read the full Privacy Policy.
